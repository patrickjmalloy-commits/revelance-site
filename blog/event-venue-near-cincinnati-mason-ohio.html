Event Venue Near Cincinnati: Why More Companies Are Choosing Mason

Revelance  ·  March 2026  ·  6 min read

If you've been searching for an event venue near Cincinnati, you've probably already run through the usual list. Downtown hotel ballrooms. Suburban banquet halls. Restaurant private rooms. A distillery that does rentals on weekends.

Most of them share the same set of problems: mandatory catering packages, AV fees that show up on the invoice after you've signed, a venue coordinator who disappears after the walkthrough, and a room that looks like it was designed to be forgettable.

There's a better option 25 minutes from downtown Cincinnati. It's in Mason, and most people in the event planning business haven't heard of it yet.

What Revelance Actually Is

Revelance is Mason, Ohio's private business club — a 25,000+ square foot standalone building at 8460 Duke Blvd designed and built specifically for the way Cincinnati-area businesses work, meet, and entertain.

It's not a converted warehouse. It's not a retail space that got a renovation. Patrick Malloy and Joe Clark took the shell of an existing building and rebuilt the entire interior from the ground up with a specific vision: a place where you can hold a board meeting in the morning, host a 150-person company event in the evening, and have your clients walking away impressed by both.

The anchor event space is the Revel Room — 125 seats banquet style, 225 guests in a combined reception configuration, 25-foot ceilings, a 12×10 foot video wall, and built-in AV that doesn't cost extra. Adjoining it is the Taproom, operated by Sonder Brewing, with a full craft beer and spirits program and a bartender included with every event booking.

What Makes It Different from Other Venues Near Cincinnati

The short version: Revelance doesn't operate like a hotel or a banquet hall, and that difference shows up in every line of your quote.

No mandatory catering. Most venues in the Greater Cincinnati area require you to use their preferred caterers or in-house food service, often with per-head minimums. At Revelance, you bring whoever you want. Your company's go-to BBQ spot. A private chef for a cooking demonstration. A food truck for a product launch. The building provides the space, the bar, and the AV. The rest is your call.

No AV surprises. The video wall, the wireless screen sharing, the microphones — they're part of the room. There's no AV package to add, no "technology fee" on the invoice, no scrambling for the right HDMI adapter the morning of your event.

The bar runs at restaurant prices. The Taproom is an actual Sonder Brewing bar, priced the same as Sonder's restaurant across the parking lot. When you book the Revel Room, your guests drink at standard menu prices. There's no event markup. A craft pour costs what a craft pour costs.

One clear quote upfront. Space rental. Bar service. AV. That's the invoice. Nothing materializes after you sign.

The Location Advantage

Mason sits at the intersection of I-71 and the Warren County growth corridor — about 25 minutes north of downtown Cincinnati and equidistant from the major corporate campuses in Blue Ash, Montgomery, and West Chester.

For Cincinnati-based companies, this matters more than it might seem. A venue that's 25 minutes from downtown with private surface parking — no garages, no meters, no shared lots with a shopping center — is a meaningfully different experience for your guests than one that requires navigating downtown parking infrastructure before they've even walked in the door.

The Revelance parking lot is private, adjacent to the building, and includes EV chargers. Your guests drive up, park, and walk in. That's the whole arrival experience.

Beyond the Event Space: The Full Building

What sets Revelance apart from a dedicated event venue is that the event space is one part of a larger building designed for business.

The same building houses four private boardrooms for pre-event or post-event meetings. The C-Suite Lounge with 50 personal bourbon lockers for client entertainment. Emergency 9, a private golf simulator and conference room that takes 60-90 minutes and converts to a genuine client experience. A robotic coffee bar. A commercial culinary lab. A dedicated coworking floor above it all.

For companies that use the building regularly — and many do — the corporate account model means boardrooms, events, coworking, and entertainment all run off a single pre-funded account. One invoice, one relationship, one building.

What Kinds of Events Work Here

The Revel Room is flexible enough to reconfigure for most corporate formats:

  • Corporate offsites and all-hands meetings — classroom or theater configuration with the video wall as your presentation surface
  • Holiday parties and company celebrations — banquet rounds, full bar, bartender included
  • Client appreciation events — reception flow, bourbon locker access, golf simulator on the same floor
  • Product launches and press events — tech-forward AV, flexible layout, catering from wherever you choose
  • Fundraisers and nonprofit galas — 125 seated, no mandatory vendor restrictions
  • Rehearsal dinners and private celebrations — intimate or full capacity, private bar, no event markup on drinks

Pricing and What to Expect

Revelance doesn't publish event pricing publicly because every event is quoted based on the date, configuration, and duration — but the process is transparent and the quote is complete. There are no add-ons that appear after you've committed.

The best way to understand the space and get accurate pricing is to schedule a walkthrough. Tours are free, take about 30 minutes, and leave most planners with a clear sense of whether this is the right fit for their event.
