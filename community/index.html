Building Anchors

The companies that anchor the building

Four established businesses have put down permanent roots at Revelance — bringing financial services, medical device innovation, foodservice expertise, and craft brewing under one roof.

Fortegic Financial Planning

A comprehensive financial planning firm serving families and businesses across every stage of life — from personal wealth management and retirement planning to business protection strategies.

Visit Fortegic
Arthrex Cincinnati Medical Devices

The Cincinnati-area sales and education team for Arthrex, a global leader in orthopedic medical devices and surgical techniques — supporting surgeons and healthcare systems across the region.

Visit Arthrex Cincinnati
Zink Foodservice Group Foodservice Equipment

Ohio's commercial foodservice equipment rep agency, connecting restaurants, institutions, and operators with the equipment lines that run professional kitchens — and home of the Zink Culinary Center inside Revelance.

Visit Zink FSG
Sonder Brewing Craft Brewery

Mason's own craft brewery, operating the Revelance Taproom on the first floor at standard bar prices with no event markup. Full-service restaurant steps away at 8584 Duke Blvd.

Visit Sonder Brewing
