Past events

A look at what's happened inside (and outside) these walls.

Hops & Holly Holiday Market

December 13, 2025 · 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM · Revelance & Sonder Taproom

Revelance and Sonder Brewing hosted a festive holiday market featuring local vendors, seasonal shopping, and activities including a cocktail-making station and eco gift-wrapping workshop. Seasonal drinks flowed from the Taproom while guests browsed handmade gifts and holiday finds throughout the venue. The day also featured two ticketed workshops: a Paint Your Own Vintage Christmas Tree class (1:00–3:00 PM) where participants created vintage-style holiday art, and a Charcuterie Class (11:00 AM–12:00 PM) where attendees learned to build and style stunning holiday boards with tips from professionals. Revelance also served as a Toys for Tots drop-off location throughout the holiday season, collecting donations through the day of the market.

Christmas in July Happy Hour

July 24, 2025 · 4:00 – 7:00 PM · Revelance

Revelance threw a summer party with a holiday twist — food, drinks, crafts, and even pictures with Santa in July. The happy hour brought members and guests together for an evening of seasonal fun in the middle of summer, complete with festive activities and Sonder craft beer on tap.

Member Appreciation Lunch

June 20, 2025 · Revelance

A tropical-themed lunch celebrating Revelance members, prepared on-site by the culinary team. The annual appreciation event gave members a chance to connect, enjoy great food, and take a well-earned break from the workday — a hallmark of what makes the Revelance community different from a typical coworking space.

Iron Chef Pizza Showdown & MADE Chamber Connections Happy Hour

March 26, 2025 · 4:00 – 6:00 PM · Revelance

Revelance hosted the MADE Chamber's Connections Happy Hour featuring an Iron Chef-style pizza showdown. Local business owners and professionals mingled over craft drinks while competing pizza creations were judged and devoured. The event showcased Revelance as a gathering place for the Mason–Deerfield business community — equal parts professional networking and genuine fun.

Next Stop Comedy Nights

Recurring · 2024–2025 · Sonder Taproom at Revelance

Next Stop Comedy brings top stand-up comedians to the Sonder Taproom at Revelance for a brand-new lineup every show. Acts featured on TV and heard on popular podcasts perform live while guests enjoy Sonder craft beer on tap. The recurring series has become a signature Revelance event, drawing audiences from across the Greater Cincinnati area for a night of laughs in an intimate venue setting.

MADE Chamber Membership Campaign Event

2024 · Revelance

Revelance hosted the MADE Chamber of Commerce's annual membership campaign event, welcoming dozens of local business owners and community leaders. The evening featured networking, volunteer recognition, and new member sign-ups. Sponsors included Benchmark Gensuite and other local businesses, and the event was celebrated as a resounding success for the Mason–Deerfield business community.

Grand Opening & Ribbon Cutting

November 2024 · Revelance

Revelance officially opened its doors at 8460 Duke Blvd with a ribbon-cutting ceremony, covered by Local 12 and Cincinnati Business Courier. Over 100 guests toured the 25,000+ sq ft facility, explored the Taproom, and celebrated the newest addition to the Deerfield Township and Mason business community.

Groundbreaking Ceremony

October 2023 · 8460 Duke Blvd Construction Site

Co-founders Patrick Malloy and Joe Clark broke ground on what would become a $5.5 million, 25,000+ sq ft private business club — alongside the Bunnell Hill Construction and MA Design teams who helped bring the vision to life. Local media was on-site as the first shovels hit dirt in the rain.
