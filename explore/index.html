Directory

Every space, in plain text.

Prefer a list to a map? Here is everything in the building, with where to learn more.

  • The Revel Room — flagship event hall; 125 banquet, 225 combined, 25-ft ceilings and a 12×10-ft video wall.
  • The Taproom — craft beer bar featuring Sonder Brewing, open to members and event guests.
  • Emergency 9 — golf-simulator suite for 25–40, with a ProTee VX simulator and 10×16-ft impact screen.
  • Four Boardrooms — Foundation, Horizon, Overlook, and the members-only Cornerstone; 6 to 14 seats with full AV.
  • C-Suite Lounge — private executive lounge with 50 personal bourbon lockers.
  • Culinary Lab — 400 sq ft Ohio Risk Level III licensed commercial kitchen for chef-led events and demos.
  • Co-Revel Floor — the dedicated second-floor coworking space, never displaced by events, with phone booths and the Co-Revel Café.
  • Memberships — Day Pass $29 · Virtual Address $95 · Co-Revel $179 · Dedicated Desk $449 · Executive Office $1,599.
