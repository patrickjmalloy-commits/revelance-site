Common Questions

Good answers for curious people

We're not a coworking space — we're a private business club. The founders built this as their own headquarters first, then opened it to the business community. That's why you'll find a craft beer taproom, 50 personal bourbon lockers, a culinary lab, and a robotic coffee bar — things you won't find at any other workspace in Ohio.

We're a standalone 25,000+ sq ft building with our own private parking lot, not a suite in a strip mall. Our coworking space in Mason lives on a dedicated second floor, completely separated from event spaces — so your workday is never interrupted when a private event is happening downstairs. At most coworking spaces, a paid event means you lose your common area.

Everything runs through the Revelance App — your phone unlocks every door (no key fobs), you book boardrooms in seconds, and you connect with other members through built-in community chat and message boards. The owners work here every day. The building is home to established businesses in finance, foodservice, and medical devices — you're never walking into an empty space.
