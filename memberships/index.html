Life as a Member

More than a desk — it's a community

The little things that make Revelance feel less like a workspace and more like your second home.

Monthly Member Buffets

Complimentary lunch spreads prepared on-site. Connect with fellow members over great food — on us.

🥘

Chef Meal Service

Our on-site chef offers prepackaged take-home meals. Skip the dinner scramble — pick up on your way out.

🍺

Taproom Access

Exclusive access to the Taproom — craft beer by Sonder Brewing, happy hours, and networking events.

🏈

Watch Parties & Events

NCAA tournaments, FIFA World Cup, Super Bowl — big screens, big sound, and a full bar.

🤝

Networking Community

Work alongside business owners, executives, and professionals. Connections happen naturally here.

📱

The Revelance App

Your phone is your key — unlock every door, book boardrooms, manage your account, and connect with other members through community chat and message boards. No key fobs, no front desk check-in.

Your Floor, Your Space

Co-Revel and workspace live on the dedicated second floor — completely separated from event spaces below. When a 200-person gala is happening downstairs, your workday isn't interrupted. At other coworking spaces, a paid event means you lose your common area.

Surprise & Delight

From tastings to impromptu happy hours — the owners are on-site daily making members' days better.
