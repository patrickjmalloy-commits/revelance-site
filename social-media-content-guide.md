## HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Each blog post has been broken into social media hooks — short, punchy versions of the core arguments. Each hook includes a LinkedIn version (longer, professional), an Instagram/Facebook version (shorter, conversational), and a suggested CTA.

Post 2-3x per week. Rotate between categories. Every post should link back to the blog or site.

## QUICK TIPS

1. **Lead with the pain point, not the feature.** "What happens when someone books the event space?" hits harder than "We have two separate floors."
2. **Every post needs one clear CTA.** Book a tour. Try a free day. Read the full post. Pick one.
3. **Photos > stock graphics.** Use Phil Armstrong's photography. The building sells itself visually.
4. **The Taphouse and bourbon lockers are your most shareable assets.** They stop the scroll.
5. **Tag Sonder Brewing** in taphouse content. Their audience overlaps with yours.
6. **Repost member content.** If a member posts from the space, share it. Social proof > marketing copy.
