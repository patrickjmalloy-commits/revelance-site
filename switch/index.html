Offer terms

The fine print, in plain language

  • Available to new Revelance members who hold an active paid membership at another coworking space at the time of signup.
  • Proof required: an invoice, receipt, or statement from your current coworking provider dated within 60 days of your Revelance signup.
  • After six consecutive months of paid Revelance membership in good standing, you receive two months of account credit applied to months 7 and 8 at your plan's monthly rate.
  • Applies to any membership tier. One offer per member or company. Cannot be combined with other offers or discounts.
  • Revelance may modify or end this offer for new signups at any time; active enrollees keep their terms.
  • Questions? Call 513-794-5070 or email info@revelanceoh.com.
